How to collect

  1. Enter a public address above. That is all the tool ever asks for.
  2. Open the position on Uniswap's own interface with the link beside it.
  3. Press Collect there and sign with your own wallet. The fees go straight to that wallet.

Collecting always goes through Uniswap's official page and pays 100% to your own wallet. We never take a cut.

One collect takes everything. Uniswap settles the fees still accruing inside the pool at the same moment, so a single transaction is enough — you do not need to decrease liquidity first.

Safety

Money that nobody has claimed attracts people who want to take it. Three rules cover almost every attack:

  1. Never share a seed phrase or private key. No tool, and no member of any team, will ever need it.
  2. Never approve a contract to "unlock" or "release" fees. Collecting is an ordinary transaction you sign yourself, on Uniswap.
  3. No one will message you first with a claim link. If someone DMs you about this page, it is not us.

We will never DM you first, never ask for a seed phrase, and never send claim links.

Anyone can copy a website. A clone of this page exists only to get a signature out of you. This tool never asks you to connect a wallet, so if any page built to look like it does, close the tab. Check the address bar first.
